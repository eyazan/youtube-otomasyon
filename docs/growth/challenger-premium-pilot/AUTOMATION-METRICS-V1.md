**What is genuinely automated:**
- **Of the Challenger planner shots:** 75% are generated with no human edits; the camera, timing and transition values in them are 100% generated.
- **Of the overall production:** about 0% is automated end to end. Approval, voice and anchors remain human gates, and no engine-generated video has been approved.

## Quality gates (not weakened)

All existing gates are unchanged:
- validator;
- text gate (0 FAIL / 0 WARN on the production plan);
- creative QA;
- technical QA.

Gates added this round:
- verified-film-segment rule for film clocks;
- camera position checks;
- channel label minimum (36 px);
- digit-boundary display check (a correctness fix).
