An avalanche can be triggered by one footstep and move faster than a car. Here is the hidden science under the snow. The failure mechanism: weak-layer collapse under a snow slab.

Failure chain: Weak layer buried → Slab loads on top → Trigger: skier or new snow → Weak layer collapses → Slab releases
