Ajka flooded villages with caustic red mud. The cause: structural failure of a reservoir dyke holding highly alkaline bauxite residue.

Failure chain: Red mud stored behind dyke → Wall weakens → Corner breaches → Caustic mud floods villages → Rivers contaminated
