USAir 1016 crashed into trees and a house near Charlotte. The cause: microburst wind shear on approach, with alerts that came late or on another frequency.

Failure chain: Storm over the airport → Warning on another frequency → Microburst on approach → Go-around too late → Jet hits trees and house
